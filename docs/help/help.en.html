Program Purpose

The program is designed for learning to tell the time in foreign languages and for training listening comprehension.

The application shows times in the HH:MM format, says them aloud in the selected language with the selected voice, and plays a whole list of times one after another with a pause between them.

Use it to train your ear for the way times are said in different languages: for example, «quarter to four», «Viertel vor vier» or «без четверти четыре».

Interface

  • Help (this window)
  • Settings window: the same settings as at the bottom of the main page
  • Colour mode of the interface: contrast, normal, evening, night
  • Time fields: a random time button 🎲, the time (H:MM or HH:MM, from 0:00 to 23:59) and the play button ▶️ for this time
  • Rnd: fills all fields with random times (minutes in steps of 5)
  • Start / Stop / Continue: plays the times one after another; the field being played is highlighted
  • Reset: returns the exercise to the beginning (available when it is paused)

Settings

  • Speed: the speed of the speech
  • Delay (ms): the pause between the times when the list is played
  • Language and Voice: the language of the speech and the voice of your device (the list depends on your device and browser)
  • Interface: the language of the application
  • Theme and Mode: the colours of the application
  • Reset defaults: returns all settings to the initial values

Notes

  • The settings are saved in your browser.
  • If the language has no voices on your device, install a voice in the system settings.
  • This help can be shown in any language: select it in the field above.
