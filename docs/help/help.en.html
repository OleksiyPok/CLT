Program Purpose

Clock Listening Trainer is designed for learning to tell the time in foreign languages and for training listening comprehension.

The program shows times in the HH:MM format, says them aloud in the selected language with the selected voice, and plays a whole list of times one after another with a pause between them.

Use it to train your ear for the way times are said in different languages: for example, «quarter to four», «Viertel vor vier» or «без четверти четыре».

The interface is available in 13 languages: العربية, Čeština, Deutsch, English, Español, Français, Italiano, Nederlands, Polski, Português, Русский, Türkçe, Українська.

Main screen

  1. Information: opens this window.
  2. Settings: opens the settings window (see "Settings").
  3. Mode: colour mode of the interface: Contrast, Normal, Evening or Night. Each press switches to the next mode.
  4. Time fields: a random time button 🎲, the time (H:MM or HH:MM, from 0:00 to 23:59) and the play button ▶︎ for this time.
  5. Rnd: fills all fields with random times (minutes in steps of 5).
  6. Start / Pause / Continue: plays the times one after another; the field being played is highlighted.
  7. Reset: returns the exercise to the beginning (available when it is paused).
  8. Playback controls: Speed, Interval, Language and Voice. They are the same controls as in the settings window and are described in "Settings".

How to use

  1. Choose the language and the voice in the settings.
  2. Press 🎲 to get a random time in a field, type a time yourself (for example 14:25) or press Rnd to fill all fields.
  3. Press ▶︎ to listen to one time, or Start to listen to all the times one after another.
  4. Write down what you hear and compare it with the time in the field.

Settings

The settings window is opened with the button. It consists of blocks; scrolling takes you from block to block, and a fraction in the corner shows the number of the current block over the total number of blocks. The playback controls of the main screen are the same controls and always show the same values. A change is applied at once, even during playback.

Speed and interval

  1. Speed: the speed of the speech, from 0.6 (slow) to 2.0 (fast).
  2. Interval (ms): the pause between the end of one time and the beginning of the next when the list is played, in milliseconds.

Rows

  1. Rows: how many time fields there are on the main screen, from 1 to 15.

Language and voice

  1. Language: the language of the speech.
  2. Voice: the voice of your device. The list depends on your device and browser.

Interface, theme and mode

  1. Interface: the language of the program labels.
  2. Theme: the colour palette of the program.
  3. Mode: Contrast (brighter and sharper), Normal, Evening or Night. Night mode is dark and dim, so you can look at the screen calmly in the dark. It works in sync with the Mode button on the main screen.

Reset defaults

  1. Reset defaults (): returns all settings to their original values.

Notes

All settings are saved in your browser and are kept after you close the program.

The help and settings windows are closed with the ✕ button or the Esc key; on a phone, also with the Back button. The F1 key opens this window.

If the language has no voices on your device, install a voice in the system settings.
