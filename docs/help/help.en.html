Program Purpose

The program is designed for learning to tell the time in foreign languages and for training listening comprehension.

The application shows times in the HH:MM format, says them aloud in the selected language with the selected voice, and plays a whole list of times one after another with a pause between them.

Use it to train your ear for the way times are said in different languages: for example, «quarter to four», «Viertel vor vier» or «без четверти четыре».

Main screen

  1. Help (this window)
  2. Settings window: the same settings as at the bottom of the main page
  3. Colour mode of the interface: contrast, normal, evening, night
  4. Time fields: a random time button 🎲, the time (H:MM or HH:MM, from 0:00 to 23:59) and the play button ▶️ for this time
  5. Rnd: fills all fields with random times (minutes in steps of 5)
  6. Start / Stop / Continue: plays the times one after another; the field being played is highlighted
  7. Reset: returns the exercise to the beginning (available when it is paused)
  8. Settings: speed, delay, language and voice, interface, theme and mode (the same settings are in the settings window)

How to practise

  1. Choose the language and the voice in the settings.
  2. Press 🎲 to get a random time in a field, type a time yourself (for example 14:25) or press Rnd to fill all fields.
  3. Press ▶️ to listen to one time, or Start to listen to all the times one after another.
  4. Write down what you hear and compare it with the time in the field.

Settings

  1. Speed: the speed of the speech
  2. Interval (ms): the pause between the times when the list is played
  3. Language and Voice: the language of the speech and the voice of your device (the list depends on your device and browser)
  4. Interface: the language of the application
  5. Theme and Mode: the colours of the application
  6. Reset defaults: returns all settings to the initial values

Notes

The settings are saved in your browser.

If the language has no voices on your device, install a voice in the system settings.
